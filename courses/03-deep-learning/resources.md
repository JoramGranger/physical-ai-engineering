- [Stanford CS231n, 2025](https://cs231n.stanford.edu/2025/) — CNN and visual recognition context.
- [Deep Learning](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/) — Neural-network theory and optimization.
- [PyTorch tutorials](https://docs.pytorch.org/tutorials/) — Implementation, autograd, and profiling documentation.
