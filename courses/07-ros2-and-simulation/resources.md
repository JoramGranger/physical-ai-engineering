# Resources and reading selections

- [ROS 2 Jazzy docs](https://docs.ros.org/en/jazzy/) — Official Jazzy documentation; content browsing blocked during verification, see central register.
- [Gazebo Harmonic / ROS compatibility](https://gazebosim.org/docs/harmonic/ros_installation/) — Verified Jazzy/Harmonic compatibility and setup guidance.
- [Modern Robotics textbook](https://modernrobotics.org/) — Mechanics and transform reasoning.
- [Northwestern Modern Robotics videos](https://modernrobotics.northwestern.edu/nu-gm-book-resource/) — Physical interpretation of robot models.

Select the syllabus topics within these sources; the labels are our study route, not asserted lecture titles or numbers.
