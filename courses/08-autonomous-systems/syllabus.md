# Autonomous Systems syllabus

Study these units in order during learning months 7–8. Units describe conceptual order, not additional calendar weeks. Reuse the monthly assignment and avoid starting a separate project for every topic.

| Unit | Topics and subtopics | Evidence of understanding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Estimation | EKF/particle filtering, wheel/IMU fusion, time alignment, observability. |
| 2 | Mapping and odometry | VO, scan matching, SLAM, loop closure, map-based localization. |
| 3 | Motion planning | A*/sampling-based concepts, collision inflation, trajectory tracking, Nav2. |
| 4 | Aerial simulation | PX4 SITL, frames, offboard/mission control, command-loss response. |

## Completion standard

Submit the [core assignment](assignments/README.md), exercise reasoning, and a short note explaining one failure case. Apply the [curriculum assessment policy](../../docs/curriculum.md#assessment-policy). Reproduce a small case before increasing data, model, or robot complexity.
