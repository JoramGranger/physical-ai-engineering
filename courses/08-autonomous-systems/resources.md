# Resources and reading selections

- [Princeton Introduction to Robotics](https://irom-lab.princeton.edu/intro-to-robotics/) — Robot autonomy and planning context.
- [MIT Underactuated Robotics](https://underactuated.mit.edu/) — Control and trajectory optimization selections.
- [Probabilistic Robotics](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262201629/probabilistic-robotics/) — State estimation and mapping textbook.
- [Nav2 Jazzy](https://docs.nav2.org/jazzy/) — Jazzy mobile navigation integration.
- [PX4 v1.17](https://docs.px4.io/v1.17/en/) — Versioned aerial simulation and behavior documentation.

Select the syllabus topics within these sources; the labels are our study route, not asserted lecture titles or numbers.
