# Edge AI and Deployment syllabus

Study these units in order during learning months Parallel throughout; focused integration in 11–12. Units describe conceptual order, not additional calendar weeks. Reuse the monthly assignment and avoid starting a separate project for every topic.

| Unit | Topics and subtopics | Evidence of understanding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Reference contracts | Inputs, preprocessing, versions, output semantics, test fixtures. |
| 2 | Export and parity | ONNX, opsets, shape constraints, numerical/task-level comparison. |
| 3 | Optimization | Precision, representative calibration, TensorRT compatibility, memory/power/thermal behavior. |
| 4 | Deployment and operation | Serving queues, health, observability, artifact promotion, rollback and fallback. |

## Completion standard

Submit the [core assignment](assignments/README.md), exercise reasoning, and a short note explaining one failure case. Apply the [curriculum assessment policy](../../docs/curriculum.md#assessment-policy). Reproduce a small case before increasing data, model, or robot complexity.
