# Resources and reading selections

- [Northwestern Modern Robotics videos](https://modernrobotics.northwestern.edu/nu-gm-book-resource/) — Mechanics, planning, and control video selections.
- [Princeton Introduction to Robotics](https://irom-lab.princeton.edu/intro-to-robotics/) — Robotics modeling and system context.
- [MIT Underactuated Robotics](https://underactuated.mit.edu/) — Dynamics and control reference.
- [Modern Robotics textbook](https://modernrobotics.org/) — Core robotics textbook.

Select the syllabus topics within these sources; the labels are our study route, not asserted lecture titles or numbers.
