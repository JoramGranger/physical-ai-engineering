# Robotics Engineering syllabus

Study these units in order during learning months 5, with capstone reuse. Units describe conceptual order, not additional calendar weeks. Reuse the monthly assignment and avoid starting a separate project for every topic.

| Unit | Topics and subtopics | Evidence of understanding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Kinematics | Frames, forward/inverse kinematics, Jacobians, workspace, singularities. |
| 2 | Dynamics and actuation | Joint torques, simplified motor models, friction, saturation. |
| 3 | Control | PID, timestep selection, anti-windup, disturbances, trajectories. |
| 4 | C++ and embedded interfaces | RAII, build tooling, timing, encoders, watchdog, telemetry. |

## Completion standard

Submit the [core assignment](assignments/README.md), exercise reasoning, and a short note explaining one failure case. Apply the [curriculum assessment policy](../../docs/curriculum.md#assessment-policy). Reproduce a small case before increasing data, model, or robot complexity.
