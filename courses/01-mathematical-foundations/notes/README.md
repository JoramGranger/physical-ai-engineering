# Original study notes

Develop these two notes as you work; they are writing prompts, not completed lessons.

- Explain the distinction between a mathematical inverse and solving a linear system.
- Document a finite-difference failure caused by cancellation or a nondifferentiable point.

For each note include: question, assumptions, symbols/units, derivation or argument, a small worked case, verification evidence, a failure case, and source attribution. Record unresolved questions rather than presenting guesses as established facts. Link the resulting note from your [journal](../../../docs/learning-journal.md).
