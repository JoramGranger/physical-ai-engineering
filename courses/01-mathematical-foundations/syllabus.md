# Mathematical Foundations syllabus

Study these units in order during learning months 1, with refreshers in 4–10. Units describe conceptual order, not additional calendar weeks. Reuse the monthly assignment and avoid starting a separate project for every topic.

| Unit | Topics and subtopics | Evidence of understanding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Linear algebra and conditioning | Least-squares solve using QR/SVD; identify rank deficiency. |
| 2 | Calculus and numerical derivatives | Shape-aware loss derivation and finite-difference comparison. |
| 3 | Probability and estimation | Conditional model, likelihood, sample covariance, and uncertainty interpretation. |
| 4 | Optimization and generalization | Step-size experiment and explanation of regularization. |

## Completion standard

Submit the [core assignment](assignments/README.md), exercise reasoning, and a short note explaining one failure case. Apply the [curriculum assessment policy](../../docs/curriculum.md#assessment-policy). Reproduce a small case before increasing data, model, or robot complexity.
