# Lecture study route

Use the [official sources](../resources.md) in this order. This is an original topic guide; no local lecture recordings or copied decks are supplied.

1. **Linear algebra and conditioning.** Read/watch selected material, then produce: Least-squares solve using QR/SVD; identify rank deficiency.
2. **Calculus and numerical derivatives.** Read/watch selected material, then produce: Shape-aware loss derivation and finite-difference comparison.
3. **Probability and estimation.** Read/watch selected material, then produce: Conditional model, likelihood, sample covariance, and uncertainty interpretation.
4. **Optimization and generalization.** Read/watch selected material, then produce: Step-size experiment and explanation of regularization.

Spend the curriculum's reading allocation on the topic you cannot yet explain or verify. After a session, write the key assumption and a question answered by the next exercise. Do not infer access to a restricted recording from a public course homepage.
