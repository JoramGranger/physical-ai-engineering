# Resources and reading selections

- [MIT 6.801 Machine Vision](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-801-machine-vision-fall-2020/) — Classical image formation and processing; OCW notes/assignments.
- [Stanford CS231n, 2025](https://cs231n.stanford.edu/2025/) — Deep visual recognition.
- [Szeliski — Computer Vision](https://szeliski.org/Book/) — Vision reference across classical and learned methods.
- [OpenCV 4.x](https://docs.opencv.org/4.x/) — Processing, video, and coordinate APIs.

Select the syllabus topics within these sources; the labels are our study route, not asserted lecture titles or numbers.
