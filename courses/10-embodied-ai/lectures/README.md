# Lecture study route

Use the [official sources](../resources.md) in this order. This is an original topic guide; no local lecture recordings or copied decks are supplied.

1. **Decision processes.** Read/watch selected material, then produce: MDP/POMDP, observation/state, action rates, reward and termination.
2. **Imitation learning.** Read/watch selected material, then produce: Demonstration provenance, behavior cloning, covariate shift, episode splits.
3. **Reinforcement learning.** Read/watch selected material, then produce: Bellman backups, small value/policy baseline, seed and reward diagnostics.
4. **Multimodal action models.** Read/watch selected material, then produce: VLM versus VLA, action tokens/normalization/horizons, domain and compute limits.

Spend the curriculum's reading allocation on the topic you cannot yet explain or verify. After a session, write the key assumption and a question answered by the next exercise. Do not infer access to a restricted recording from a public course homepage.
