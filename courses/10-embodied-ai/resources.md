# Resources and reading selections

- [Berkeley CS185/285](https://rail.eecs.berkeley.edu/deeprlcourse/) — RL and imitation-learning university selections.
- [MIT Underactuated Robotics](https://underactuated.mit.edu/) — Dynamics, optimization, and control context.
- [OpenVLA research project](https://openvla.github.io/) — Concrete VLA research example; reproduction is optional.
- [Deep Learning](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/) — Optimization and representation support.
- [Modern Robotics textbook](https://modernrobotics.org/) — Action and robot-mechanics interface.

Select the syllabus topics within these sources; the labels are our study route, not asserted lecture titles or numbers. Use the [central verification register](../../docs/resources.md) for the 2026-11-23 access review, restricted-video caveats, and book availability. External resources retain their own licenses; link rather than redistribute.
