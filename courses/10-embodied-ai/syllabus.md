# Embodied AI syllabus

Study these units in order during learning months 10. Units describe conceptual order, not additional calendar weeks. Reuse the monthly assignment and avoid starting a separate project for every topic.

| Unit | Topics and subtopics | Evidence of understanding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Decision processes | MDP/POMDP, observation/state, action rates, reward and termination. |
| 2 | Imitation learning | Demonstration provenance, behavior cloning, covariate shift, episode splits. |
| 3 | Reinforcement learning | Bellman backups, small value/policy baseline, seed and reward diagnostics. |
| 4 | Multimodal action models | VLM versus VLA, action tokens/normalization/horizons, domain and compute limits. |

## Completion standard

Submit the [core assignment](assignments/README.md), exercise reasoning, and a short note explaining one failure case. Apply the [curriculum assessment policy](../../docs/curriculum.md#assessment-policy). Reproduce a small case before increasing data, model, or robot complexity.
