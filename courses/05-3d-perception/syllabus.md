# 3D Perception and Spatial Intelligence syllabus

Study these units in order during learning months 4. Units describe conceptual order, not additional calendar weeks. Reuse the monthly assignment and avoid starting a separate project for every topic.

| Unit | Topics and subtopics | Evidence of understanding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Camera models | Intrinsics, extrinsics, lens distortion, calibration views. |
| 2 | Two-view geometry | Correspondence, epipolar constraints, robust estimation, triangulation. |
| 3 | Depth and reconstruction | Stereo disparity, RGB-D backprojection, point clouds, scale ambiguity. |
| 4 | Alignment and error | ICP assumptions, bundle-adjustment objective, residuals and degeneracy. |

## Completion standard

Submit the [core assignment](assignments/README.md), exercise reasoning, and a short note explaining one failure case. Apply the [curriculum assessment policy](../../docs/curriculum.md#assessment-policy). Reproduce a small case before increasing data, model, or robot complexity.
